Job Title: Machine Learning Engineer

Location: Remote / Hybrid / On-site (flexible)

About the Company:
We are a fast-growing technology team building intelligent products that solve real-world problems. Our cross-functional teams combine research, software engineering, and design to deliver production-grade ML systems that impact thousands of users.

Role Summary:
We are looking for an experienced Machine Learning Engineer to design, build, and ship scalable machine learning models and data-driven features into production. You will partner with product managers, data engineers, and software engineers to transform data into reliable ML services that power user-facing products and internal tools.

Key Responsibilities:
- Design, develop, and maintain end-to-end machine learning models and pipelines for production systems.
- Collaborate with data engineering to collect, clean, and validate datasets; implement feature engineering and labeling strategies.
- Prototype models quickly, evaluate performance using rigorous metrics, and iterate to production quality.
- Implement training, validation, and hyperparameter tuning workflows; ensure reproducibility and experiment tracking.
- Deploy and monitor models in production (APIs, batch jobs, streaming); build alerts and telemetry to track model health and data drift.
- Optimize models and inference pipelines for latency, throughput, and cost at scale.
- Work with software engineers to integrate models into services, and maintain CI/CD for ML (MLOps).
- Conduct A/B tests and post-deployment analysis to measure impact and guide improvements.
- Document designs, experiments, and operational runbooks; mentor junior engineers as needed.

Required Qualifications:
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, Mathematics, Statistics, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.
- 3+ years of experience building and shipping ML models or data-driven features in production.
- Strong programming skills in Python and familiarity with core ML libraries (scikit-learn, TensorFlow, PyTorch).
- Hands-on experience with data processing tools and libraries (pandas, NumPy, SQL); comfortable working with large datasets.
- Proven experience deploying models to production using containers, orchestration (Docker, Kubernetes), or serverless platforms.
- Solid understanding of core ML fundamentals: supervised/unsupervised learning, model evaluation, regularization, feature selection, and overfitting mitigation.
- Experience with experiment tracking, model versioning, and reproducibility tools (MLflow, TensorBoard, DVC, or similar).
- Familiarity with cloud platforms (AWS, GCP, or Azure) and common services for data storage, compute, and ML workloads.
- Strong communication skills and ability to collaborate with cross-functional teams.

Preferred Qualifications:
- MS or PhD in a relevant field.
- Experience with deep learning for NLP, computer vision, or recommendation systems.
- Background in scalable data processing frameworks (Spark, Beam) and real-time/streaming systems.
- Knowledge of model interpretability, fairness, privacy-preserving ML, and causal inference.
- Published research or contributions to open-source ML projects.

Technical Skills & Tools (examples):
- Languages: Python, SQL
- Frameworks: PyTorch, TensorFlow, scikit-learn
- Data: pandas, NumPy, Spark
- Infrastructure: Docker, Kubernetes, Airflow, Terraform
- Cloud: AWS (S3, EC2, SageMaker), GCP (BigQuery, Dataflow), or Azure equivalents
- MLOps: MLflow, DVC, Seldon, KServe, Prometheus, Grafana
